Quant — separate and removable

First conjunctive prefix/suffix/contains/ndim match wins, else default. Everything goes through gguf.quants.quantize; packing errors propagate. No catch, retry, Q4_K_M recipe, imatrix, or CPU dequant. Native Vulkan upload retained; F16 3D conv expansion retained; GgufFile::floats requires F32. Shipped rules: scripts/quant_t3.json (prefixes chatterbox/, voice_encoder/, model/wpe, model/ln_f/, model/norm/, model/rope_freq_factors; suffix /b, /g; ndim 1) and scripts/quant_s3.json (prefixes flow/input_embedding, flow/spk_embed_affine/, s3gen/builtin/, s3gen/mel_fb/, campplus/, s3tokv2/, cfm/, hift/; contains /attn/pos_bias_; suffix /b; ndim 1, 3, 4). CLI type is the default; --t3-quant-policy / --s3-quant-policy replace rules. Names: chatterbox-t3-{variant}-{default}-{sha8}.gguf, chatterbox-s3gen-{family}-{default}-{sha8}.gguf, sha8 = sha256 of canonical rules JSON. quant.py plus the two JSONs are the whole mechanism.
